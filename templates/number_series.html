Number Series

Learn to identify patterns and find missing numbers in a sequence.

📘 Concept Summary

Number series consists of numbers arranged according to a specific rule. The task is to identify the pattern and find the next or missing term.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Numbers may follow addition or subtraction patterns.
  • Multiplication and division patterns are common.
  • Square and cube patterns are also used.
  • Some series follow alternate patterns.

⭐ Important Patterns

Arithmetic Pattern:
2, 5, 8, 11...
(Common Difference = 3)
Multiplication Pattern:
2, 4, 8, 16...
(Multiply by 2)
Square Pattern:
1, 4, 9, 16...

💡 Tricks

  • Find difference between consecutive numbers.
  • Check multiplication or division.
  • Look for alternate term patterns.

📝 Example

Find the next number:
3, 6, 12, 24, ?

Each number is multiplied by 2.

3 × 2 = 6
6 × 2 = 12
12 × 2 = 24

Next term = 48
