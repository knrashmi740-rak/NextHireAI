Ratio & Proportion

Understand comparison between quantities and their relationships.

📘 Concept Summary

Ratio compares two quantities using division. Proportion shows equality between two ratios. These concepts are widely used in mixtures, partnership and age problems.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Ratio is represented as a:b.
  • Both quantities in a ratio should have the same units.
  • Proportion means two ratios are equal.
  • If a:b = c:d, then a,d are extremes and b,c are means.

⭐ Important Formulas

Ratio = First Quantity / Second Quantity
If a:b = c:d

Then a × d = b × c
Fourth Proportion = (Second × Third) / First

💡 Shortcuts & Tricks

  • Always convert quantities into the same units.
  • Simplify ratios using HCF.
  • Use cross multiplication for proportion problems.
  • Ratios can be increased or decreased by multiplying both terms with the same number.

📝 Example

The ratio of boys to girls is 2:3. If boys are 20, find girls.

2 : 3 = 20 : x

x = (20 × 3) / 2

x = 30
