Time Speed Distance

Learn the relationship between speed, distance and time.

📘 Concept Summary

Time Speed Distance problems are based on the movement of objects. These concepts are used in trains, vehicles, boats and daily life problems.

⭐ Important Formulas

Distance =

Speed × Time
Speed =

Distance / Time
Time =

Distance / Speed

💡 Shortcuts & Tricks

  • Convert km/hr into m/sec:
    × 5/18
  • Convert m/sec into km/hr:
    × 18/5
  • Speed and time are inversely proportional.
  • Always maintain the same units.

📝 Example

A car travels 120 km in 3 hours. Find speed.

Speed = Distance / Time

= 120 / 3

= 40 km/hr
