Simple Interest

Learn how interest is calculated on the original principal amount.

📘 Concept Summary

Simple Interest is the amount paid or earned on the original amount of money called principal. It is commonly used in banking and aptitude problems.

⭐ Important Terms

  • Principal (P): Initial amount of money.
  • Rate (R): Percentage of interest per year.
  • Time (T): Duration for which money is invested.
  • Amount: Total money after adding interest.

⭐ Important Formulas

Simple Interest (SI) =

(P × R × T) / 100
Amount =

Principal + Simple Interest
P = (SI × 100) / (R × T)

💡 Shortcuts & Tricks

  • Always convert rate into percentage.
  • Time should be taken in years.
  • For months, convert months into years.
  • Simple interest is always calculated on the original principal.

📝 Example

Find SI on ₹5000 at 10% per year for 2 years.

SI = (5000 × 10 × 2)/100

= ₹1000
