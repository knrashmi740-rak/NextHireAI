Pipes & Cisterns

Learn problems related to filling and emptying tanks using pipes.

📘 Concept Summary

Pipes and Cisterns problems are similar to Time and Work. Here pipes fill or empty a tank instead of completing work.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Filling pipes increase water level.
  • Emptying pipes decrease water level.
  • Combined efficiency determines total filling time.

⭐ Important Formulas

Work done by filling pipe = 1 / Time
Work done by emptying pipe = -1 / Time
Total Work = Sum of individual work rates

💡 Tricks

  • Take filling pipes as positive.
  • Take emptying pipes as negative.
  • Use LCM method for faster calculation.

📝 Example

Pipe A fills a tank in 10 hours and Pipe B fills it in 15 hours. Find time taken together.

A = 1/10

B = 1/15

Total = 1/10 + 1/15

= 1/6

Time taken = 6 hours
