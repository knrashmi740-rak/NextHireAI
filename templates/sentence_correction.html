Sentence Correction

Learn how to identify and correct grammatical mistakes in sentences.

📘 Concept Summary

Sentence Correction questions test grammar rules, vocabulary and sentence structure. The goal is to find the correct form of a sentence.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Check subject-verb agreement.
  • Check tense consistency.
  • Check articles and prepositions.
  • Check word usage and sentence structure.

⭐ Important Methods

Incorrect:
He don't like coffee.

Correct:
He doesn't like coffee.
Incorrect:
She is more smarter.

Correct:
She is smarter.

💡 Tricks

  • Read the complete sentence.
  • Find grammar errors step by step.
  • Check whether meaning remains same after correction.

📝 Example

Correct the sentence:
He have completed his work.

Subject = He (singular)
Correct verb = has

Answer:
He has completed his work.
