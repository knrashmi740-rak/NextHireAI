One Word Substitution

Learn how to replace phrases with a single meaningful word.

📘 Concept Summary

One Word Substitution questions test vocabulary by replacing a group of words with one suitable word.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • A phrase can represent one specific meaning.
  • Vocabulary knowledge is important.
  • Common words are frequently asked in placement exams.

⭐ Important Methods

A person who writes poems:
Poet
A place where books are kept:
Library
A person who cannot read or write:
Illiterate

💡 Tricks

  • Learn common exam vocabulary lists.
  • Understand the meaning of the phrase.
  • Practice previous year questions.

📝 Example

A person who loves mankind.

Meaning:
Someone who cares for humanity.

Answer = Philanthropist
