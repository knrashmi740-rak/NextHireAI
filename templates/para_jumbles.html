Para Jumbles

Learn how to arrange sentences in the correct logical order.

📘 Concept Summary

Para Jumbles are questions where sentences of a paragraph are given in a random order. The task is to arrange them to form a meaningful paragraph.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Find the opening sentence of the paragraph.
  • Identify connecting words between sentences.
  • Look for cause and effect relationships.
  • Maintain the logical flow of ideas.

⭐ Important Methods

Steps:

1. Find the introductory sentence.
2. Find sentences that continue the idea.
3. Connect examples and explanations.
4. Find the conclusion.

💡 Tricks

  • A sentence starting with "This, These, They" usually follows a previous sentence.
  • The first sentence usually introduces the topic.
  • The final sentence gives a conclusion.

📝 Example

Arrange the sentences:

A. He planted many trees.
B. Ravi loves nature.
C. The trees helped the environment.

Correct Order:

B → A → C

Explanation:
First introduce Ravi, then action, then result.
