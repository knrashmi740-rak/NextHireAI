Prepositions

Learn the correct usage of words that show relationships between objects and actions.

📘 Concept Summary

Prepositions are words used before nouns or pronouns to show position, direction, time, or relationship between words in a sentence.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Prepositions show place, time and direction.
  • Common prepositions are in, on, at, by, for, from, to, with.
  • Correct preposition usage improves sentence accuracy.
  • Some verbs require fixed prepositions.

⭐ Important Methods

Place:

In → Inside something
On → Surface
At → Specific point
Time:

In → Months / Years
On → Days / Dates
At → Exact time

💡 Tricks

  • Remember common word combinations.
  • Check whether the sentence talks about time, place or direction.
  • Practice commonly confused prepositions.

📝 Example

Fill the blank:
She is good ___ mathematics.

The correct preposition with "good" is "at".

Answer = She is good at mathematics.
