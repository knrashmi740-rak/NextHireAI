Error Detection

Learn to identify grammatical mistakes in sentences.

📘 Concept Summary

Error detection questions require finding incorrect grammar usage in a sentence and correcting it.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Check subject-verb agreement.
  • Check tense errors.
  • Check article usage.
  • Check preposition mistakes.
  • Check singular and plural forms.

⭐ Important Methods

Wrong:
She go to school every day.

Correct:
She goes to school every day.
Wrong:
He is senior than me.

Correct:
He is senior to me.

💡 Tricks

  • Read the sentence slowly.
  • Check each part separately.
  • Focus on common grammar rules.

📝 Example

Find the error:
They was playing cricket.

Subject = They (plural)
Correct verb = were

Answer = They were playing cricket.
