Blood Relations

Learn how to identify relationships between family members using logical reasoning.

📘 Concept Summary

Blood Relation questions test your ability to determine the relationship between two or more family members based on the given information.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Understand family relationships clearly.
  • Draw a family tree whenever possible.
  • Identify generations correctly.
  • Pay attention to gender and relationship words.

⭐ Important Methods

Father's Brother = Uncle
Mother's Brother = Maternal Uncle
Brother's Son = Nephew
Sister's Daughter = Niece

💡 Tricks

  • Draw a simple family tree.
  • Mark male and female members.
  • Solve one relationship at a time.
  • Don't skip generations.

📝 Example

Ravi is the brother of Sita. Sita is the mother of Arun. How is Ravi related to Arun?

Ravi → Brother of Sita
Sita → Mother of Arun

Mother's Brother = Uncle

Answer = Uncle
