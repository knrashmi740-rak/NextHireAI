Simplification & Approximation

Learn quick calculation methods using basic mathematical rules.

📘 Concept Summary

Simplification involves solving mathematical expressions using rules like BODMAS, fractions, percentages and approximations.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • BODMAS: Bracket, Order, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction.
  • Fractions and decimals conversion.
  • Square and cube calculations.
  • Approximation for faster solving.

⭐ Important Rules

(a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
(a-b)² = a² - 2ab + b²
Percentage = Value / Total × 100

💡 Shortcuts & Tricks

  • Follow BODMAS order strictly.
  • Learn squares up to 30 for faster calculations.
  • Use approximation when options are far apart.
  • Avoid unnecessary long calculations.

📝 Example

Solve: 25 + 5 × 10

First multiplication:

5 × 10 = 50

25 + 50 = 75
