Seating Arrangement

Learn how to arrange people logically according to given conditions.

📘 Concept Summary

Seating Arrangement questions require arranging people in a row or around a circle by following the given clues.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Linear arrangement.
  • Circular arrangement.
  • Left and right positions.
  • Facing inside or outside.

⭐ Important Methods

Linear Arrangement

A B C D E
Facing North
Left = Left
Right = Right
Facing South
Left and Right are Reversed

💡 Tricks

  • Draw empty positions first.
  • Place fixed positions before others.
  • Use elimination method.
  • Verify all given conditions.

📝 Example

Five people A, B, C, D and E sit in a row. A sits in the middle. B sits to the left of A. C sits to the right of A.
