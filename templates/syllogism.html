Syllogism

Learn how to analyze statements and draw logical conclusions.

📘 Concept Summary

Syllogism questions consist of statements followed by conclusions. The task is to determine whether the conclusion logically follows from the given statements.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Statements provide the given information.
  • Conclusions must be checked only using given statements.
  • Venn diagrams help solve complex problems.
  • Avoid using outside knowledge.

⭐ Important Methods

All A are B
A is completely inside B
No A are B
A and B have no common area
Some A are B
A and B have partial overlap

💡 Tricks

  • Draw Venn diagrams for better understanding.
  • Check every conclusion separately.
  • Do not assume extra information.
  • Look for words like All, Some, No.

📝 Example

Statement:
All cats are animals.
All animals are living beings.

Conclusion:
All cats are living beings.

Cats → Animals → Living Beings

Conclusion follows.

Answer = True
