Direction Sense

Learn how to determine directions, positions and shortest distances.

📘 Concept Summary

Direction Sense questions test your ability to determine the final direction or position after a series of movements.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Know the four main directions.
  • Understand left and right turns.
  • Track every movement carefully.
  • Use diagrams whenever possible.

⭐ Important Methods

North ↑
South ↓
East →
West ←
Right Turn: North → East → South → West
Left Turn: North → West → South → East

💡 Tricks

  • Draw the path step by step.
  • Mark every turn immediately.
  • Find the final direction first.
  • Use Pythagoras theorem for shortest distance.

📝 Example

A person walks 5 km North and then 3 km East. Find the shortest distance.

Distance

√(5² + 3²)

= √34

≈ 5.83 km
