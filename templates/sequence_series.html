Sequence & Series

Learn number patterns, arithmetic progression and geometric progression problems commonly asked in placement aptitude tests.

📘 Concept Summary

A sequence is an arrangement of numbers in a specific order following a particular pattern. A series is the sum of terms of a sequence.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • A sequence contains terms arranged according to a rule.
  • The difference between consecutive terms is called common difference.
  • The ratio between consecutive terms is called common ratio.
  • Arithmetic Progression (AP) uses addition pattern.
  • Geometric Progression (GP) uses multiplication pattern.

⭐ Important Formulas

Arithmetic Progression (AP):

a, a+d, a+2d, a+3d...
Nth term of AP:

aₙ = a + (n-1)d
Sum of AP:

Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n-1)d]
Geometric Progression (GP):

a, ar, ar², ar³...
Nth term of GP:

aₙ = arⁿ⁻¹

💡 Tricks

  • Find the difference between consecutive numbers first.
  • For multiplication patterns, check common ratio.
  • Look for alternating patterns in complex series.
  • Break large sequences into smaller patterns.

📝 Example

Find the next number in the sequence:
2, 5, 8, 11, ?

Difference between terms:

5 - 2 = 3

8 - 5 = 3

11 - 8 = 3

Next number = 11 + 3

Answer = 14

📝 Example 2

Find the next term:
3, 6, 12, 24, ?

Each number is multiplied by 2

3 × 2 = 6
6 × 2 = 12
12 × 2 = 24

Next term = 48
