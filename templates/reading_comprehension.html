Reading Comprehension

Learn techniques to understand passages and answer questions accurately.

📘 Concept Summary

Reading Comprehension questions are based on understanding a given passage, finding main ideas and answering questions from the text.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Read the passage carefully.
  • Identify the main idea.
  • Understand keywords and important points.
  • Answer only using information from the passage.

⭐ Important Methods

Reading Strategy:

1. Read the questions first.
2. Read the passage.
3. Locate keywords.
4. Select the correct answer.

💡 Tricks

  • Do not assume information.
  • Focus on facts mentioned in the passage.
  • Improve reading speed.

📝 Example

Passage says:
"Trees provide oxygen and protect the environment."

Question:
Why are trees important?

Answer:
Trees provide oxygen and protect the environment.
