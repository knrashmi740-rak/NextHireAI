Statement & Conclusion

Learn how to identify valid conclusions from given statements.

📘 Concept Summary

Statement and Conclusion questions test logical thinking by checking whether a conclusion follows from the given statement. The conclusion should be based only on the information provided.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • A statement contains given information or facts.
  • A conclusion is an inference drawn from the statement.
  • Do not use personal assumptions while solving.
  • A conclusion must logically follow the statement.

⭐ Important Methods

Statement → Given Information
Conclusion → Result Based on Statement
Check:
Does the conclusion directly follow?
Yes = Valid
No = Invalid

💡 Tricks

  • Read the statement carefully.
  • Avoid outside knowledge.
  • Check every conclusion separately.
  • Look for words like all, some, only, always.

📝 Example

Statement:
All students must attend the exam.

Conclusion:
Students should prepare for the exam.

Statement talks about exam attendance.

Preparation is a logical assumption but not directly given.

Answer = Conclusion does not follow
