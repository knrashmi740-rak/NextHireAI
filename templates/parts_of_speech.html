Parts of Speech

Learn the basic categories of words used in English sentences.

📘 Concept Summary

Parts of Speech are the different types of words classified according to their function in a sentence. Understanding them helps in improving grammar and sentence formation.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Noun - Name of a person, place, thing or idea.
  • Pronoun - Used instead of a noun.
  • Verb - Shows action or state.
  • Adjective - Describes a noun.
  • Adverb - Describes a verb, adjective or another adverb.
  • Preposition - Shows relationship between words.
  • Conjunction - Joins words or sentences.
  • Interjection - Shows sudden feelings.

⭐ Important Methods

Example:

Ravi plays cricket well.

Ravi → Noun
plays → Verb
cricket → Noun
well → Adverb

💡 Tricks

  • Identify the role of the word in the sentence.
  • Check whether the word represents action, name or description.
  • Practice sentence examples regularly.

📝 Example

Find the adjective:
"The beautiful flower bloomed."

Flower is a noun.
Beautiful describes the flower.

Answer = Beautiful (Adjective)
