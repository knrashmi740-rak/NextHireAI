Synonyms & Antonyms

Improve vocabulary by learning similar and opposite meanings of words.

📘 Concept Summary

Synonyms are words with similar meanings, while antonyms are words with opposite meanings.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Synonym = Similar meaning word.
  • Antonym = Opposite meaning word.
  • Vocabulary knowledge improves accuracy.
  • Context of the sentence is important.

⭐ Important Methods

Synonyms:

Happy = Glad
Big = Large
Antonyms:

Hot ≠ Cold
Increase ≠ Decrease

💡 Tricks

  • Learn new words daily.
  • Understand meaning instead of memorizing only.
  • Practice words used in exams.

📝 Example

Find synonym of "Rapid".

Rapid means fast.

Answer = Quick
