Idioms & Phrases

Learn common expressions and their meanings used in English.

📘 Concept Summary

Idioms are expressions where the meaning is different from the actual words. Phrases are groups of words that express a particular idea.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Idioms cannot be understood by only individual word meanings.
  • They have fixed meanings.
  • Frequently asked in placement exams.
  • Context helps to understand meaning.

⭐ Important Methods

A piece of cake

Meaning:
Something very easy
Hit the nail on the head

Meaning:
To say exactly the right thing

💡 Tricks

  • Learn commonly used idioms.
  • Understand the situation where it is used.
  • Practice previous exam questions.

📝 Example

Meaning of "Break the ice":

To start a conversation and make people comfortable.

Answer = Begin interaction
