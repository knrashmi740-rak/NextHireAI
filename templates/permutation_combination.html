Permutation & Combination

Learn arrangements and selections of objects using formulas and shortcuts.

📘 Concept Summary

Permutation and Combination are used to count the number of possible arrangements and selections. Permutation focuses on order, while Combination focuses on selection.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Permutation: Arrangement where order matters.
  • Combination: Selection where order does not matter.
  • Factorial (n!): Product of all numbers from 1 to n.

⭐ Important Formulas

Factorial:

n! = n × (n-1) × (n-2) ... × 1
Permutation:

nPr = n! / (n-r)!
Combination:

nCr = n! / r!(n-r)!

💡 Shortcuts & Tricks

  • If order matters → Use Permutation.
  • If order does not matter → Use Combination.
  • Remember: nCr = nC(n-r).
  • Always simplify factorials before calculating.

📝 Example

How many ways can 3 students be selected from 5 students?

5C3 = 5! / 3!2!

= 10 ways
