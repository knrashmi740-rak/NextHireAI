Profit & Loss

Understand buying, selling, profit, loss and discount concepts used in aptitude exams.

📘 Concept Summary

Profit and Loss problems are based on the difference between Cost Price and Selling Price. These concepts are commonly asked in placement tests.

⭐ Important Terms

  • Cost Price (CP): Price at which an item is purchased.
  • Selling Price (SP): Price at which an item is sold.
  • Marked Price (MP): Original labelled price.
  • Discount: Reduction in marked price.

⭐ Important Formulas

Profit = SP - CP
Loss = CP - SP
Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100
Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100
Discount = MP - SP

💡 Tricks

  • SP greater than CP means profit.
  • CP greater than SP means loss.
  • Profit and loss percentage is always calculated on CP.
  • For discounts, percentage is calculated on MP.

📝 Example

A product is bought for ₹500 and sold for ₹600. Find profit percentage.

Profit = 600 - 500

= ₹100

Profit % = 100/500 ×100

= 20%
