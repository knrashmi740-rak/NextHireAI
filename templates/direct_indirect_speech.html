Direct & Indirect Speech

Learn how to convert spoken words from direct form to indirect form.

📘 Concept Summary

Direct speech gives the exact words spoken by a person. Indirect speech reports the meaning of the spoken words without using the exact sentence.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Direct speech uses quotation marks.
  • Indirect speech changes the sentence structure.
  • Pronouns may change during conversion.
  • Tense changes according to reporting verb.

⭐ Important Methods

Direct:
He said, "I am happy."

Indirect:
He said that he was happy.
Present → Past
is/am/are → was/were
will → would

💡 Tricks

  • Identify the reporting verb first.
  • Check tense changes carefully.
  • Change pronouns according to meaning.

📝 Example

Convert:
Ravi said, "I play cricket."

Reporting verb = said

Indirect form:
Ravi said that he played cricket.
