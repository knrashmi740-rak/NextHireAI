Average

Learn how to calculate averages and solve placement problems quickly.

📘 Concept Summary

Average represents the central value of a group of numbers. It helps in finding the overall performance or typical value of data.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Average is the equal distribution of total values.
  • Average lies between the highest and lowest values.
  • Adding the same number to every value increases average by that number.

⭐ Important Formulas

Average = Sum of Observations / Number of Observations
Sum of Observations = Average × Number of Observations
New Average =
(New Total Value) / (New Number of Values)

💡 Shortcuts & Tricks

  • Find total first when average is given.
  • Use deviation method for large numbers.
  • If one value changes, calculate the difference from old average.
  • Average of consecutive numbers is the middle number.

📝 Example

Find the average of 10, 20, 30.

Average = (10+20+30)/3

= 60/3

= 20
