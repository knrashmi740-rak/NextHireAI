Tenses

Learn how verbs change according to time in English grammar.

📘 Concept Summary

Tenses show the time of an action. They are divided into Present, Past and Future tense with different forms.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Present tense describes current actions.
  • Past tense describes completed actions.
  • Future tense describes upcoming actions.
  • Each tense has Simple, Continuous, Perfect and Perfect Continuous forms.

⭐ Important Methods

Present Simple:
Subject + V1 + Object

Example:
She writes a letter.
Past Simple:
Subject + V2 + Object

Example:
She wrote a letter.
Future Simple:
Subject + will + V1 + Object

Example:
She will write a letter.

💡 Tricks

  • Look for time indicators like yesterday, tomorrow, now.
  • Identify the verb form.
  • Understand the action timing.

📝 Example

Choose the correct tense:
I ____ to college every day.

Regular action → Present tense

Answer = I go to college every day.
