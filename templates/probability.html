Probability

Learn the basics of probability, formulas and shortcuts used in aptitude exams.

📘 Concept Summary

Probability is the measure of how likely an event is to happen. It is used in aptitude questions involving coins, dice, cards and random events. The value of probability always lies between 0 and 1.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Experiment: An action that produces outcomes.
  • Outcome: Result obtained from an experiment.
  • Sample Space: Set of all possible outcomes.
  • Event: A specific outcome or group of outcomes.

⭐ Important Formulas

Probability of an Event =

Number of Favorable Outcomes / Total Number of Outcomes
Probability of Impossible Event = 0
Probability of Certain Event = 1
P(A) + P(Not A) = 1

💡 Shortcuts & Tricks

  • Probability value is always between 0 and 1.
  • For dice problems, total outcomes = 6.
  • For two dice, total outcomes = 36.
  • Always identify total possible cases before solving.

📝 Example

Find the probability of getting an even number on a dice.

Favourable outcomes = 2,4,6

Total outcomes = 6

Probability = 3/6

= 1/2
