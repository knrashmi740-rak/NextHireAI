Letter Series

Learn alphabet-based patterns and find missing letters in sequences.

📘 Concept Summary

Letter series questions contain alphabets arranged according to a specific pattern. The goal is to identify the rule and find the missing letter.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Alphabet positions are important.
  • Forward and backward movements are used.
  • Some series skip letters with fixed gaps.
  • Combination of letters and numbers can appear.

⭐ Important Methods

A=1, B=2, C=3 ...... Z=26
Forward Movement: A → C → E → G (Gap of 2 letters)
Backward Movement: Z → X → V → T

💡 Tricks

  • Convert letters into numbers.
  • Find the gap between letters.
  • Check alternate letter patterns.

📝 Example

Find the missing letter:
A, C, E, G, ?

Alphabet positions:

A = 1
C = 3
E = 5
G = 7

Next position = 9

Answer = I
