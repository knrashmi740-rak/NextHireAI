Articles

Learn the correct usage of A, An and The in English sentences.

📘 Concept Summary

Articles are words used before nouns to show whether we are talking about something specific or general.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • A is used before words beginning with consonant sounds.
  • An is used before words beginning with vowel sounds.
  • The is used for specific nouns.
  • Articles are used before singular countable nouns.

⭐ Important Methods

A:
A book
A university
An:
An apple
An hour
The:
The Sun
The Taj Mahal

💡 Tricks

  • Focus on pronunciation, not only spelling.
  • Use "an" before vowel sounds.
  • Use "the" when something is already known.

📝 Example

Fill the blank:
She is ___ honest person.

Honest starts with a vowel sound.

Answer = She is an honest person.
