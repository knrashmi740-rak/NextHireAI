Cloze Test

Learn how to complete passages by selecting suitable words.

📘 Concept Summary

Cloze Test consists of a passage with missing words. You need to choose the most suitable word based on grammar and meaning.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Vocabulary knowledge is important.
  • Understand the complete passage.
  • Check grammar and sentence meaning.
  • Consider the context before choosing an answer.

⭐ Important Methods

Steps:

1. Read the complete passage.
2. Understand the topic.
3. Fill easy blanks first.
4. Check whether the sentence makes sense.

💡 Tricks

  • Do not choose words only by meaning.
  • Check tense and grammar.
  • Read the sentence again after filling the blank.

📝 Example

The sun ___ in the east.

Options:
A) rise
B) rises
C) rising

Answer = rises

Reason:
Sun is singular and the sentence is in simple present tense.
