Analogy

Learn how to find relationships between words, numbers and objects.

📘 Concept Summary

Analogy questions are based on identifying the relationship between two objects and applying the same relationship to another pair.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Words can have similar relationships.
  • Numbers can follow mathematical patterns.
  • Objects can be related by function or category.
  • Identify the first pair relationship carefully.

⭐ Important Methods

A : B :: C : ?
A is related to B
C follows the same relation
Example:
Bird : Fly
Fish : Swim

💡 Tricks

  • Find the exact relation between the first pair.
  • Check meaning before choosing answers.
  • Look for category, function or similarity.
  • Avoid choosing based only on appearance.

📝 Example

Book : Read :: Food : ?

Book is used for reading.

Food is used for eating.

Answer = Eat
