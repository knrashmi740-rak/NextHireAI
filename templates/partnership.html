Partnership

Learn how partners share profit based on investment and time period.

📘 Concept Summary

Partnership problems are based on the amount of money invested by partners and the duration for which the money is invested. Profit sharing depends on capital and time.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • More investment gives more profit share.
  • Investment for a longer period gives higher profit.
  • Profit sharing ratio depends on Capital × Time.

⭐ Important Formulas

Profit Ratio = Investment × Time
A : B = (A's Capital × A's Time) : (B's Capital × B's Time)
Individual Profit = Total Profit × Individual Ratio / Total Ratio

💡 Tricks

  • Convert months into the same unit.
  • Multiply investment with time before finding ratio.
  • Simplify the final ratio.

📝 Example

A invests ₹5000 for 12 months and B invests ₹8000 for 6 months. Find the profit sharing ratio.

A : B

= 5000 × 12 : 8000 × 6

= 60000 : 48000

= 5 : 4
