Problems on Ages

Learn how to solve age-based problems using equations and relationships.

📘 Concept Summary

Age problems involve finding present, past and future ages of people. These problems are solved by creating equations based on given conditions.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Age difference between two people always remains constant.
  • Future age is calculated by adding years.
  • Past age is calculated by subtracting years.
  • Unknown ages are represented using variables.

⭐ Important Formulas

Future Age = Present Age + Number of Years
Past Age = Present Age - Number of Years
Age Difference = Older Age - Younger Age

💡 Tricks

  • Assume unknown age as x.
  • Convert statements into equations.
  • Use age difference instead of calculating every age.

📝 Example

A father is 3 times as old as his son. After 10 years, father will be twice the son's age. Find their present ages.

Let son's age = x

Father's age = 3x

After 10 years:

3x + 10 = 2(x + 10)

3x + 10 = 2x + 20

x = 10

Son's age = 10 years
Father's age = 30 years
