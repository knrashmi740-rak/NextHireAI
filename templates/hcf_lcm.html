HCF & LCM

Learn how to find Highest Common Factor and Least Common Multiple using different methods.

📘 Concept Summary

HCF (Highest Common Factor) is the largest number that divides two or more numbers exactly. LCM (Least Common Multiple) is the smallest number that is a common multiple of given numbers.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • HCF is also called Greatest Common Divisor (GCD).
  • LCM is the smallest common multiple of numbers.
  • HCF is usually smaller than the given numbers.
  • LCM is usually greater than or equal to the given numbers.

⭐ Important Formulas

For two numbers: HCF × LCM = Product of two numbers
LCM = (First Number × Second Number) / HCF
HCF is found by common prime factors

💡 Tricks

  • Use prime factorization for large numbers.
  • Use division method for quick HCF calculation.
  • For fractions, first convert them into common form.

📝 Example

Find HCF and LCM of 12 and 18.

12 = 2 × 2 × 3

18 = 2 × 3 × 3

HCF = 2 × 3 = 6

LCM = (12 × 18) / 6

= 36
