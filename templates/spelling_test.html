Spelling Test

Learn how to identify correct spellings and avoid common spelling mistakes.

📘 Concept Summary

Spelling Test questions check your ability to identify correctly spelled words and find spelling errors.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Learn commonly confused spellings.
  • Understand silent letters.
  • Pay attention to vowel combinations.
  • Practice frequently asked words.

⭐ Important Methods

Common Mistakes:

Wrong: Accomodation
Correct: Accommodation
Wrong: Seperate
Correct: Separate

💡 Tricks

  • Break long words into smaller parts.
  • Remember common prefixes and suffixes.
  • Read English regularly.

📝 Example

Find the correct spelling:

A) Enviroment
B) Environment

Answer = Environment
