Sentence Completion

Learn how to choose the correct word to complete sentences.

📘 Concept Summary

Sentence completion questions test grammar, vocabulary and understanding of sentence meaning.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Read the complete sentence first.
  • Understand the meaning and tone.
  • Choose words that fit grammatically.
  • Eliminate incorrect options.

⭐ Important Methods

Example:

She was tired, ____ she continued working.

Correct word:
but

💡 Tricks

  • Look for connecting words.
  • Check tense consistency.
  • Understand the sentence meaning.

📝 Example

He is very honest and always speaks the ____.

Correct word:
truth

Answer = He always speaks the truth.
