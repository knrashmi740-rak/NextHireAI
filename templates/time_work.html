Time & Work

Learn how to calculate work efficiency, time taken and combined work.

📘 Concept Summary

Time and Work problems are based on the relationship between work done, number of workers and time required.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Work = Efficiency × Time
  • More efficiency means less time required.
  • When people work together, their efficiencies are added.

⭐ Important Formulas

Work = Rate × Time
If A completes work in x days, One day work of A = 1/x
Combined Work = A's work + B's work
Total Time = Total Work / Efficiency

💡 Tricks

  • Convert every person's work into one-day work.
  • LCM method helps in faster calculations.
  • Efficiency and time are inversely proportional.

📝 Example

A can complete a work in 10 days. How much work does A complete in one day?

One day work = 1/10

So in 5 days = 5/10 = 1/2 work
