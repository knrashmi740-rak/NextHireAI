Active & Passive Voice

Learn how sentences change when focus shifts from subject to action.

📘 Concept Summary

Active voice focuses on the person performing the action. Passive voice focuses on the action or the object receiving the action.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Active voice has subject performing the action.
  • Passive voice has subject receiving the action.
  • Verb form changes during conversion.
  • Only transitive verbs can usually be changed into passive voice.

⭐ Important Methods

Active:
Subject + Verb + Object

Example:
Ravi wrote a letter.
Passive:
Object + helping verb + V3 + by Subject

A letter was written by Ravi.

💡 Tricks

  • Find the object first.
  • Change main verb into past participle form.
  • Maintain the original tense.

📝 Example

Convert into passive voice:
She completed the project.

Object = The project

Passive:
The project was completed by her.
