Subject Verb Agreement

Learn how subjects and verbs should match correctly in sentences.

📘 Concept Summary

Subject Verb Agreement means the subject and verb in a sentence must agree in number. Singular subjects take singular verbs and plural subjects take plural verbs.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Singular subject → Singular verb.
  • Plural subject → Plural verb.
  • Words between subject and verb do not affect agreement.
  • Collective nouns require special attention.

⭐ Important Methods

Singular:
He plays cricket.

Plural:
They play cricket.
Each / Every / Everyone
Always use singular verb.

💡 Tricks

  • Find the main subject first.
  • Ignore extra words between subject and verb.
  • Check singular and plural forms.

📝 Example

Choose correct option:
The list of items ___ on the table.

Subject = List (singular)

Correct verb = is

Answer = The list of items is on the table.
