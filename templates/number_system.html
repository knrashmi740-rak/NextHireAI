Number System

Learn different types of numbers, properties, formulas and shortcuts required for aptitude exams.

📘 Concept Summary

Number System is the foundation of quantitative aptitude. It deals with different types of numbers and their properties. Understanding number systems helps in solving problems related to divisibility, remainders, factors and multiples.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Natural Numbers: Positive counting numbers (1,2,3...)
  • Whole Numbers: Natural numbers including zero (0,1,2,3...)
  • Integers: Positive and negative numbers including zero
  • Prime Numbers: Numbers having only two factors (1 and itself)
  • Composite Numbers: Numbers having more than two factors

⭐ Important Formulas

Sum of first n natural numbers =

n(n+1)/2
Sum of first n odd numbers =

n²
Sum of first n even numbers =

n(n+1)
Product of two numbers =

HCF × LCM

💡 Shortcuts & Tricks

  • Divisibility by 2 → Last digit should be even.
  • Divisibility by 5 → Last digit should be 0 or 5.
  • Divisibility by 3 → Sum of digits should be divisible by 3.
  • Use prime factorization to find HCF and LCM quickly.

📝 Example

Find the sum of first 10 natural numbers.

n(n+1)/2

= 10 × 11 / 2

= 55
