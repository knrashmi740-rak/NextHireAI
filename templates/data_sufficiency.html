Data Sufficiency

Learn how to decide whether the given information is enough to answer a question.

📘 Concept Summary

Data Sufficiency questions provide a question followed by statements. The task is to determine whether the available data is sufficient to find the answer.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • The goal is not to solve completely but to check sufficiency.
  • Each statement must be checked separately.
  • Sometimes both statements together are required.
  • Extra calculations are usually unnecessary.

⭐ Important Methods

Statement 1 Alone
Enough information?
Statement 2 Alone
Enough information?
Both Statements Together
Required information?

💡 Tricks

  • Do not waste time finding the final answer.
  • Check whether the information is complete.
  • Analyze statements one by one.
  • Use elimination method.

📝 Example

Question:
What is the age of Ravi?

Statement 1: Ravi is 5 years older than Amit.
Statement 2: Amit is 20 years old.

Statement 1 alone: Not sufficient.

Statement 2 alone: Not sufficient.

Together: Ravi = 25 years.

Answer = Both statements required.
