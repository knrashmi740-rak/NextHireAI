Mixtures & Alligation

Learn how to solve mixture problems using average value and alligation methods.

📘 Concept Summary

Mixtures and Alligation deals with combining two or more quantities with different prices, concentrations or qualities.

⭐ Important Concepts

  • Mixture contains two or more components.
  • Alligation helps find the ratio of mixing quantities.
  • Average value helps find the final mixture price.

⭐ Important Formulas

Average Value = Total Value / Total Quantity
Cheaper Quantity : Dearer Quantity
=
Difference between Mean and Dearer : Difference between Mean and Cheaper

💡 Tricks

  • Use alligation method for price problems.
  • Keep units same before solving.
  • Convert percentages into fractions.

📝 Example

Mix ₹40/kg rice and ₹60/kg rice to get ₹50/kg mixture.

40 : 60

Difference = 10 : 10

Ratio = 1 : 1
